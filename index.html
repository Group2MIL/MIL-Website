01 / EDUCATION

Knowledge is free and collectable, but education is not.

Education is the intentional process of sharing knowledge, building skills, and shaping values.

EDUCATION / ESSAY 01

Knowledge is free and collectable, but education is not.

Education is the intentional process of sharing knowledge, building skills, and shaping values. Knowledge is the collection of lessons a person earns throughout his or her education journey. So in simpler terms: education is where a person gains knowledge.

It is known that education is mostly received from schools, where compiled knowledge is brought together in one place. This is a standard considered a requirement for every person born in every generation. But not all people are lucky enough to have the money and ability to afford school.

That does not mean a person is unable or not allowed to learn. A person collects knowledge through reading and experience. Education helps people understand the world, develop skills, and adapt to changing circumstances.

The world is constantly rising and evolving. People need to learn how to adapt to an unpredictable environment. Education gives people knowledge that allows them to satisfy their curiosity and make better decisions.

Curiosity is something that can never be completely removed from the human mind. We live in a world full of uncertainty and discovery. That is why education exists: to give people the knowledge they need throughout their lives.

EDUCATION / ESSAY 02

Education and Curiosity

Education gives people an opportunity to understand the world around them. From the first questions we ask as children to the difficult problems we face as adults, curiosity encourages us to continue learning.

Learning does not stop inside a classroom. People learn from books, experiences, conversations, mistakes, observations, and the people around them.

A curious person does not simply accept information. Instead, curiosity encourages a person to ask why, how, and what could happen next.

Education provides the foundation that allows curiosity to become understanding. It helps us turn questions into knowledge and knowledge into meaningful action.

EDUCATION / ESSAY 03

Learning to Adapt

The world is constantly changing. New technologies, new discoveries, and new challenges require people to continue learning throughout their lives.

Education prepares people to face these changes. It develops critical thinking, communication, problem-solving, and the ability to understand information.

Learning to adapt does not mean knowing everything. It means being willing to learn something new when circumstances change.

Education therefore becomes more than school. It becomes a lifelong process that allows people to grow and respond to the world around them.

HEALTH / ESSAY 01

The Body We Call Home

Health is one of the most important parts of our lives. There is a saying that our health is our wealth because no amount of money or material success can replace good health once it is lost. Health is not simply about being free from diseases or illnesses. It also means taking care of our physical, mental, and emotional well-being.

When we are healthy, we are more capable of doing the things we need and want to do. We can study better, spend time with our loved ones, participate in activities, and enjoy the simple moments of everyday life.

As students, we often become busy with school activities, assignments, examinations, projects, and other responsibilities. Because of this, taking care of ourselves can sometimes become less important.

We may skip meals because we are rushing to school, stay awake late to finish an assignment, or spend hours using our phones instead of resting. Although these may seem like small things, our everyday habits can affect our health not only in the present but also in the future.

One of the most important ways to take care of our physical health is through proper nutrition. Our bodies need different nutrients to grow, produce energy, and function properly.

Eating a variety of nutritious foods, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and sources of protein, can help our bodies stay strong. It is also important to limit foods that contain too much sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats.

For students, eating properly can also affect how we perform in school. It can be difficult to concentrate when we are hungry or when our bodies do not have enough energy.

Simple habits such as eating breakfast, drinking enough water, and not skipping meals can already make a difference. These may seem like ordinary choices, but when we practice them consistently, they become part of a healthier lifestyle.

Physical activity is another important part of maintaining a healthy body. According to the World Health Organization, children and adolescents should get an average of at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity every day.

Exercise can strengthen our muscles and bones, improve our fitness, and support our mental health. It also gives us a chance to move after spending long hours sitting in classrooms or using electronic devices.

Being physically active does not always require going to a gym or following a difficult workout. Walking, dancing, playing sports, riding a bicycle, stretching, or doing household chores can help us stay active.

Sleep is also an important part of physical health. Students sometimes sacrifice sleep because of schoolwork, social media, entertainment, or other responsibilities.

Our bodies need enough rest to recover. Lack of sleep can leave us tired and unfocused, making it harder to study and complete our responsibilities.

Maintaining good personal hygiene is another simple way of protecting our health. Bathing regularly, washing our hands, brushing our teeth, wearing clean clothes, and keeping our surroundings clean can help prevent illnesses.

Taking care of our bodies is not about becoming perfect. It is about learning to listen to what our bodies need and making better choices whenever we can.

Our body is the home that carries us through every experience in life. We depend on it every day, yet we sometimes forget to care for it until something goes wrong.

By eating properly, staying active, getting enough rest, and practicing good hygiene, we are showing ourselves that our health matters.

HEALTH / ESSAY 02

What the Mind Carries, the Body Feels

When people talk about health, they often think about the physical body. We think about eating nutritious food, exercising, getting enough sleep, and avoiding illnesses.

However, health is more than what we can see. A person may look completely healthy on the outside while struggling emotionally or mentally. This is why taking care of our mental and emotional well-being is just as important as taking care of our physical health.

Students experience different kinds of pressure every day. We have assignments to finish, examinations to prepare for, projects to complete, and expectations that we try to meet.

Aside from school, we may also have responsibilities at home, problems with friendships, family concerns, and personal struggles.

According to the World Health Organization, anxiety and depression are among the common mental health concerns among adolescents. This shows that mental health should not be treated as something unimportant.

Feeling overwhelmed does not mean that a person is weak. Everyone experiences difficult moments, and having negative emotions is a normal part of being human.

What matters is how we respond to these feelings. Instead of pretending that everything is fine, we should learn to recognize when we are struggling and find healthy ways to cope.

One way to care for our mental health is by giving ourselves time to rest. Students sometimes feel guilty when they take a break because they believe they should always be productive.

However, resting does not mean that we are being lazy. Our minds also need time to recover. Taking a short break between school tasks, getting enough sleep, listening to music, watching something we enjoy, or simply spending quiet time alone can help us recharge.

Having people we can talk to is also important. When problems become difficult to handle, talking to a trusted friend, family member, teacher, counselor, or professional can provide support.

We should also learn to recognize when other people may be struggling. Not everyone shows their emotions in the same way.

Physical health and mental health are also connected. When we are stressed, we may have difficulty sleeping, lose our appetite, or feel physically tired.

Social media can also affect how young people view themselves. We constantly see other people's achievements, appearances, relationships, and lifestyles online.

Taking care of our mental and emotional health does not mean that we will never feel stressed or sad. Life will always have difficult moments. Instead, it means learning how to handle these moments in a healthy way and knowing when we need support.

Health is something we cannot always see. A person's greatest struggle may be hidden behind a smile, a good grade, or a normal-looking day.

HEALTH / ESSAY 03

Ordinary Things, Healthier Lives

Good health does not always require big changes. In many cases, it is built through the small choices we make every day.

Drinking enough water, eating a proper meal, getting enough sleep, moving our bodies, keeping ourselves clean, and taking breaks may seem like ordinary actions.

However, when these choices become consistent habits, they can have a lasting effect on our health.

As students, it can be difficult to maintain healthy habits because of our busy schedules. We have classes, assignments, projects, examinations, extracurricular activities, and responsibilities at home.

One simple habit that can improve our health is eating properly. Food gives us the energy we need to study, move, and complete our daily responsibilities.

Developing the habit of eating balanced meals can help our bodies receive the nutrients they need.

Physical activity is another habit that we can include in our everyday routine. According to the World Health Organization, children and adolescents should get an average of at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity every day.

Walking, playing sports, dancing, riding a bicycle, stretching, or doing household chores can already help us stay active.

Our use of technology is also something we should pay attention to. Phones, computers, and other devices are useful for school, communication, and entertainment, but spending too much time on them can lead to long periods of sitting.

Sleep is another small habit that can make a big difference. Many students stay up late because of assignments, examinations, social media, games, or simply because they do not want the day to end.

Personal hygiene is also built through everyday habits. Washing our hands, bathing regularly, brushing our teeth, wearing clean clothes, and keeping our surroundings clean are simple actions that protect us from germs and illnesses.

Our mental health also depends on the small things we do. Taking breaks when we feel overwhelmed, spending time with people we care about, enjoying hobbies, and getting enough rest can help us manage stress.

Another important habit is learning when to ask for help. Some students try to handle everything alone because they do not want to appear weak or become a burden to others.

Healthy habits do not need to be perfect. There will be days when we make unhealthy choices, sleep late, eat too much junk food, or fail to exercise.

Health is not created by one perfect decision. It is created by the choices we continue making over time.

In the end, a healthy lifestyle is built in the little things. The choices we make today may seem small, but they can become habits that shape our future.

ENVIRONMENT / ESSAY 01

How Simple Habits Can Protect Our Everyday World

The environment is basically the foundation of everything we do. There is a lot of truth to the old idea that our planet is our ultimate bank account.

No amount of money or success can patch up a broken ecosystem, and you cannot buy back clean air and fresh water once they are gone.

Keeping the planet healthy isn't just about stopping massive ecological disasters either. It is about how we treat our neighborhoods every single day.

As students, it is so easy to get completely buried under classes, assignments, and trying to have a social life.

When you are rushing around like that, you sort of forget how connected we are to everything around us.

We grab single-use plastics because we are in a hurry, leave lights on, or throw away things that still work.

But these little everyday habits add up fast, and they shape the exact world we have to live in right now and down the road.

Watching what we buy and throw away is one of the easiest ways to start looking out for the planet.

Earth only has so many resources to go around, and it needs them to stay balanced so wildlife can survive.

Saving energy and water is another huge part of living a green life. When we use less, we help slow down climate change, preserve wild areas, and protect animals.

Being a student right now brings a ton of pressure, from fighting for good grades to feeling genuinely stressed out about the future of the world.

Fixing the planet starts with small, daily choices. Skipping plastic bags, saving water, getting involved in local projects, and spending time outside are simple steps that make a difference when everyone does them.

ENVIRONMENT / ESSAY 02

Caring for the World Around Us

The environment supports almost every part of human life. Clean air, clean water, healthy soil, forests, oceans, and natural resources all contribute to the conditions we need to live.

Protecting the environment does not always require dramatic actions. It can begin with how we treat the places we live, study, and spend time in.

Reducing unnecessary waste is one important step. Reusing containers, carrying reusable bags, donating old clothes, and repairing things instead of immediately throwing them away can reduce the amount of waste we produce.

Saving electricity and water is another way to become more responsible with natural resources. Turning off lights and appliances when they are not needed and avoiding unnecessary water use are simple actions.

Environmental responsibility also means understanding that our actions affect other people, animals, and future generations.

When communities work together, individual actions can become larger changes. Schools, families, communities, businesses, and governments all have important roles to play.

ENVIRONMENT / ESSAY 03

Small Choices, Bigger Change

Environmental protection often begins with ordinary decisions. A reusable bottle, a turned-off light, a properly disposed piece of garbage, or a walk instead of a short vehicle trip may seem insignificant.

However, when many people make responsible choices repeatedly, the combined effect can become much greater.

Students can contribute by participating in clean-up activities, planting trees, reducing plastic waste, conserving water, and encouraging others to care about their surroundings.

Young people may not have the power to solve every environmental problem immediately, but they can develop habits and knowledge that prepare them to become responsible citizens and future leaders.

Protecting the environment is not about becoming perfect. It is about recognizing that our choices matter and continuing to make better choices whenever possible.
