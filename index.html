01 — ABOUT

Learning today.
Caring for tomorrow.

The Initiative for Human and Environmental Wellbeing is a collection of student perspectives focused on the issues that shape our lives and our future.

Through essays, reflections, and autobiographies, this initiative explores the importance of education, health, and environmental responsibility. These topics are deeply connected to the lives of young people and to the communities in which we live.

The goal is not simply to discuss these issues, but to encourage awareness, responsibility, and meaningful action. Small decisions made by individuals can contribute to larger changes within our schools, communities, and society.

02 — RATIONALE

Why these topics matter.

Education gives people the knowledge and skills necessary to understand the world around them and prepare for the future. Health allows us to make use of those opportunities with strength, energy, and wellbeing.

At the same time, the environment provides the resources that make life possible. Clean air, clean water, healthy ecosystems, and natural resources are essential to every community.

Education, health, and the environment therefore cannot be treated as completely separate issues. They influence one another and collectively affect the quality of life of present and future generations.

01 — EDUCATION

Education

Education is more than academic achievement. It gives people the ability to think critically, understand different perspectives, develop skills, and contribute meaningfully to society.

STUDENT ESSAY EDUCATION

The Importance of Education

Education plays an important role in shaping who we are and who we can become. It provides us with knowledge, develops our skills, and helps us understand the world around us.

As students, education gives us opportunities to discover our interests and develop our abilities. It also teaches us lessons that go beyond textbooks, including discipline, responsibility, communication, and perseverance.

Education can also help people become more aware of the problems faced by their communities. With knowledge comes the ability to make informed decisions and participate responsibly in society.

Ultimately, education is an investment in both the individual and society. The knowledge and skills we gain today can help us create better opportunities for ourselves and contribute positively to the people around us.

02 — HEALTH

Health

Health is one of the most important parts of our lives. Taking care of our body, mind, and emotions allows us to learn, grow, and enjoy life.

STUDENT ESSAY HEALTH

Health Is Our Wealth

Health is one of the most important parts of our lives. There is a saying that our health is our wealth because no amount of money or material success can replace good health or buy it back once it is lost. Health is not only about being free from diseases or illnesses. It also includes taking care of our physical, mental, and emotional well-being. When we are healthy, we are more capable of doing the things we need and want to do. We can study better, spend time with our loved ones, participate in activities, and enjoy everyday life. As students, we often become busy with school activities, assignments, examinations, projects, and other responsibilities. Because of this, we sometimes forget to take care of ourselves. We skip meals, sleep late, spend too much time on our phones, or ignore stress because we think that schoolwork is more important. However, our everyday habits can affect our health not only in the present but also in the future.

Eating nutritious food is one of the simplest ways to maintain good health. Our bodies need different nutrients to grow, produce energy, and function properly. The World Health Organization recommends eating a variety of foods, including fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and sources of protein. It is also important to limit foods that contain too much sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats. A balanced diet can give students the energy they need to concentrate in class and participate in different activities. We can start with simple habits, such as eating breakfast, drinking enough water, adding fruits or vegetables to our meals, and avoiding skipping meals. These small choices can become part of our daily routine and contribute to better health.

Physical activity is another important part of a healthy lifestyle. According to the World Health Organization, children and adolescents should get an average of at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity every day. Exercise can help strengthen our muscles and bones, improve our fitness, and support our mental health. Being physically active also gives us an opportunity to take a break from sitting for long periods while studying or using electronic devices. Physical activity does not always have to be a serious workout or something difficult. Walking, dancing, playing sports, riding a bicycle, stretching, or even doing household chores can help us stay active.

Students also experience different kinds of pressure. Academic responsibilities, family problems, friendships, expectations, and personal struggles can sometimes become overwhelming. There are days when we may feel mentally and emotionally tired even if we are not physically exhausted. Taking care of our mental health is therefore equally important. According to the World Health Organization, anxiety and depression are among the common mental health concerns among adolescents. This shows why it is important to recognize our emotions instead of always ignoring them. Feeling stressed, worried, sad, or overwhelmed does not make someone weak. What matters is learning healthy ways to cope with these feelings. Talking to a trusted friend, family member, teacher, counselor, or other professional can provide support during difficult situations. Taking short breaks, spending time with people we care about, doing hobbies, and giving ourselves time to rest can also help us manage everyday stress.

Aside from food, exercise, mental health, and sleep, maintaining good personal hygiene is also important. Simple habits such as bathing regularly, washing our hands, brushing our teeth, keeping our surroundings clean, and wearing clean clothes can help protect us from illnesses and keep us feeling comfortable. Personal hygiene may seem like a small part of our daily routine, but it can have a big effect on our health and confidence. Developing these habits while we are young can also help us become more responsible for our own well-being.

Another important part of health is knowing when to ask for help. Sometimes, students may try to handle everything on their own because they do not want to bother other people. However, asking for help does not mean that we are incapable. Whether we are struggling with schoolwork, stress, relationships, or personal problems, reaching out to someone we trust can make difficult situations easier to handle. We should also learn to listen to our bodies and recognize when something does not feel right. Taking health concerns seriously and seeking appropriate help when needed is an important part of being responsible for ourselves.

Good health begins with small and consistent choices. We do not need to completely change our lifestyle overnight. Eating nutritious food, staying physically active, getting enough sleep, practicing good hygiene, managing stress, taking breaks, and asking for help are simple steps that can make a meaningful difference. We should remember that taking care of our health is just as important as achieving good grades. Education helps prepare us for our future, but good health gives us the strength and energy to reach our goals. By caring for ourselves today, we are also preparing ourselves for a healthier and more productive future. Health is something we should value every day because when we take care of our body, mind, and emotions, we give ourselves a better chance to enjoy life and achieve the things we dream of.

03 — ENVIRONMENT

Environment

Protecting the environment begins with understanding that every person has a responsibility to care for the world we depend on.

STUDENT ESSAY ENVIRONMENT

Our Planet, Our Responsibility

The environment is basically the foundation of everything we do. There is a lot of truth to the old idea that our planet is our ultimate bank account. No amount of money or success can completely repair a broken ecosystem, and we cannot simply buy back clean air, fresh water, healthy soil, and natural resources once they are gone. Keeping the planet healthy is not only about preventing massive ecological disasters. It is also about how we treat our surroundings every single day. Our homes, schools, communities, forests, rivers, oceans, and even the air we breathe are all connected. As students, it is easy to become completely buried under classes, assignments, examinations, projects, and trying to have a social life. When we are constantly rushing around, we sometimes forget how connected we are to everything around us. We grab single-use plastics because we are in a hurry, leave the lights on, waste water, or throw away things that could still be reused. These little everyday habits may seem unimportant, but when millions of people do them repeatedly, they can have a major effect on the environment. The choices we make today help shape the exact world we have to live in now and in the future.

Watching what we buy and throw away is one of the easiest ways to start looking out for the planet. Earth only has a limited amount of natural resources, and these resources need to be managed carefully so that people, animals, and plants can continue to depend on them. According to the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP, 2025), moving toward a lifestyle where we reuse things and reduce the amount of waste we produce can help address global pollution. This means becoming more aware of the things we purchase and asking ourselves if we actually need them before buying them. We can choose reusable products instead of constantly buying disposable ones. Bringing a reusable water bottle, using reusable bags, reusing containers, donating old clothes, and repairing damaged items instead of immediately throwing them away are simple examples. It also means being more careful about fast fashion and products that are covered in unnecessary plastic packaging. Living a greener lifestyle does not mean giving up everything we enjoy. It simply means learning to consume responsibly and choosing the more environmentally friendly option whenever we can.

Saving energy and water is another important part of living a greener life. When we use these resources responsibly, we help reduce unnecessary consumption and lessen the pressure placed on the environment. The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC, 2023) emphasizes the importance of reducing greenhouse gas emissions and using energy and resources more efficiently. We do not need to completely change our lifestyle to make a difference. We can start by turning off lights, fans, computers, and other appliances when they are not being used. We can also unplug devices when they are fully charged and use natural light whenever possible. Saving water can be as simple as turning off the faucet while brushing our teeth, taking shorter showers, fixing leaks, and avoiding unnecessary water use when cleaning. These actions may seem small, but when many people practice them consistently, the results can become much bigger.

Transportation is another area where our choices can affect the environment. Many vehicles use fossil fuels that contribute to air pollution and greenhouse gas emissions. Students can consider alternative ways of traveling whenever they are practical and safe. Walking or riding a bicycle for short distances can reduce the need for fuel while also allowing us to stay physically active. Taking public transportation or carpooling with family and friends can also reduce the number of vehicles on the road. Of course, not everyone has the same transportation options, so the goal is not to make everyone change how they travel completely. Instead, it is about being aware of our choices and making environmentally friendly decisions whenever possible.

Being a student today also brings a lot of pressure. We have academic responsibilities, deadlines, examinations, family expectations, friendships, and personal struggles to deal with. At the same time, we are constantly exposed to news about climate change, pollution, deforestation, extreme weather, and other environmental problems. Because of this, it is understandable that some young people may feel worried about the future of the planet. The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO, 2024) found that eco-anxiety is a growing mental health concern among young people. When environmental problems seem too large, it can make us feel helpless because we may think that nothing we do will actually matter. However, instead of allowing these feelings to completely discourage us, we can turn our concern into positive action. Joining a school clean-up, planting trees, helping with environmental campaigns, reducing our personal waste, or talking about environmental concerns with friends and teachers can help us feel more involved in creating solutions. We may not be able to solve every environmental problem ourselves, but we can still contribute to change.

Environmental protection also requires cooperation. One person can make responsible choices, but bigger changes become possible when families, schools, communities, businesses, and governments work together. Environmental problems do not stop at the boundaries of one house or one community. Pollution can travel through air and water, while climate change can affect people around the world. This means that everyone has a role to play. Students may not have the power to create major environmental policies yet, but they can learn, speak up, encourage others, and develop responsible habits. Young people can also become future leaders, scientists, teachers, lawyers, engineers, and other professionals who can create better solutions for environmental challenges.

Fixing the planet starts with small, daily choices. Nobody expects us to completely rewrite our whole lives overnight. Using reusable bags, reducing plastic waste, saving water, conserving electricity, properly disposing of garbage, choosing sustainable transportation when possible, joining local environmental projects, and spending time outside are simple steps that can make a difference when people practice them consistently. Taking care of the Earth is just as important as getting a perfect report card because our environment supports everything we need to live and succeed. We need clean air to breathe, clean water to drink, healthy food to eat, and safe communities where we can study, work, and grow. As students, we may sometimes think that our individual actions are too small to matter, but change often begins with small choices that are repeated and shared by many people. By looking after our world today, we are not only protecting nature but also giving ourselves and future generations a healthier, safer, and brighter future to look forward to.

CONTRIBUTOR

Jyllana Gera S. Aficial

My name is Jyllana Gera S. Aficial, and I live in Calasiao, Pangasinan. I am currently a Grade 11 student taking the STEM strand. Being a STEM student can be challenging because of all the activities, projects, and lessons that come with it, but I am doing my best to learn and improve along the way. Although I am taking STEM, I actually have a strong passion for law and hope to become a lawyer someday.

One of my biggest interests is learning about law and how it affects people and society. I find it interesting how lawyers defend people, solve problems, and stand up for what they believe is right. I like the idea of being able to help people who need someone to speak for them. Even though my current strand is STEM, I do not think it should stop me from pursuing law in the future. I believe that my experiences in STEM can also help me develop skills like critical thinking, problem-solving, and analyzing information.

Aside from academics, I have different hobbies that I enjoy. I like listening to music, watching movies and shows, scrolling through social media, and spending time with my friends and family. I also enjoy having time for myself, especially after a stressful day at school. Sometimes, I just like staying in my room, listening to music, or watching something to relax. I also enjoy talking with my friends and making random memories with them.

I would describe myself as someone who can be quiet and shy around people I do not know well, but I become more comfortable and talkative once I get close to someone. I can also be an overthinker, but I am learning how to handle things better and not let every little problem bother me too much.

As I continue my journey as a Grade 11 student, I know that there will be many challenges ahead. I may not have everything figured out yet, but I know that I want to work hard, make my family proud, and eventually pursue my passion for law. I am excited to see where my experiences take me and what kind of person I will become in the future.
